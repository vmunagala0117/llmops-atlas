# 13.2 Cancelled Recurring Transaction

## When it applies

The cardholder had an ongoing subscription, membership or free trial that turns into a paid plan, cancelled it, and was billed again after cancelling. This includes a free trial the cardholder at first did not recognise, as long as they signed up and then cancelled.

## When it does not apply

- The cardholder never signed up or gave the merchant their card: use 10.4.
- There was only ever one order, even if the merchant's site calls it a subscription: use 13.7.
- The merchant confirmed in writing that it would refund the charges after the cancellation, but the refund never arrived: use 13.6.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- The date and method of cancellation, and any cancellation confirmation.
- The dates of the charges made after cancelling.
- The terms the cardholder agreed to at sign-up, including any notice period.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder raises the dispute within 90 days of the first charge after cancelling.
