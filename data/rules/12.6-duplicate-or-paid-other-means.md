# 12.6 Duplicate Processing or Paid by Other Means

## When it applies

The cardholder made one purchase but it was charged more than once, or they already paid for it another way (cash, a different card, a voucher) and the card was charged as well.

## When it does not apply

- There is only one charge, but for the wrong amount: use 12.5. A second charge for the same amount that the merchant calls a correction is still a duplicate, so it stays 12.6.
- The cardholder does not recognise either charge: use 10.4.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- Both statement lines, with dates and amounts.
- Proof of the other payment (cash receipt, other card statement), if the cardholder paid another way.
- Confirmation from the cardholder that they made only one purchase.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder raises the dispute within 90 days of the later charge.
