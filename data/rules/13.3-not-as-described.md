# 13.3 Not as Described or Defective Merchandise or Services

## When it applies

Goods or a service arrived, but they were damaged, defective, or materially different from what the merchant described, and the merchant has not fixed it.

## When it does not apply

- Nothing arrived at all: use 13.1. If a package arrived but held the wrong or a cheaper item, something did arrive, so it stays 13.3.
- The cardholder returned the item and the merchant confirmed a refund in writing, but it never arrived: use 13.6.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- The product listing or description at the time of purchase.
- Photos of the item received, or a description of the fault.
- The cardholder's attempt to return the item or ask for a repair or exchange.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder raises the dispute within 90 days of receiving the goods or service.
