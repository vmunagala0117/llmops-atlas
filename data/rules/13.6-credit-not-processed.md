# 13.6 Credit Not Processed

## When it applies

The merchant confirmed in writing (an email, a credit slip, a refund confirmation) that it would refund or credit the cardholder, but the credit never reached the card. The reason for the refund does not matter: a lost order, a return or a cancellation.

## When it does not apply

- The merchant only mentioned a refund, offered one on conditions, or promised one by phone, and the goods never arrived: use 13.1.
- The cardholder cancelled a one-off order and was charged, with no written refund confirmation: use 13.7.
- The cardholder cancelled a subscription and was billed again, with no written refund confirmation: use 13.2.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- The written refund or credit confirmation, with its date and amount.
- Proof of return, if the refund depended on returning goods.
- Statement lines showing no matching credit.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder waits 15 days after the confirmation date for the credit, then raises the dispute within 90 days of the confirmation date.
