# 12.5 Incorrect Amount

## When it applies

The cardholder made the purchase and agrees there is one charge, but the amount charged differs from the amount they agreed to, for example the receipt, quote or menu price.

## When it does not apply

- The same purchase was charged more than once: use 12.6. If there is only one charge on the statement, it is 12.5 even when the cardholder first thought they were charged twice.
- The merchant agreed to refund part of the amount but the refund never arrived: use 13.6.
- The cardholder did not make the purchase at all: use 10.4.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- The receipt, invoice or quote showing the agreed amount.
- The statement line showing the amount charged.
- Any tip or extra charge the cardholder agreed to, if the merchant says it explains the difference.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder raises the dispute within 90 days of the transaction date.
