# 13.1 Merchandise or Services Not Received

## When it applies

The cardholder paid for goods or a service that never arrived or was never provided, and the merchant has not confirmed a refund in writing. A refund that was only mentioned, offered on conditions or promised by phone does not count as confirmed.

## When it does not apply

- The merchant confirmed a refund or credit in writing, but it never reached the card: use 13.6.
- Something arrived, but it was damaged, defective or not what was ordered (including a different, cheaper model): use 13.3.
- The cardholder cancelled a one-off order before it was delivered and was still charged: use 13.7.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- The order confirmation with the promised delivery date.
- Tracking information, and any proof of delivery the merchant holds.
- The cardholder's messages to the merchant asking about the order.
- Any written refund confirmation, which would point to 13.6 instead.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder waits until the promised delivery date has passed, then raises the dispute within 90 days of that date.
