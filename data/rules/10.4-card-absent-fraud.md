# 10.4 Other Fraud: Card-Absent Environment

## When it applies

The cardholder says they did not make or authorise the purchase, and the card was not physically present: an online, phone or mail order. The claim is about who made the purchase, not about what was bought.

## When it does not apply

- The cardholder signed up for a free trial or subscription, even one they forgot, and billing continued after they cancelled: use 13.2. Forgetting a sign-up is not the same as never authorising it.
- The cardholder made the purchase but was charged twice for it: use 12.6.
- The cardholder made the purchase but the amount is wrong: use 12.5.
- The card was present (chip, swipe or tap in a shop). That is a different fraud code, outside the 8 codes Atlas uses.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- Confirmation that the card is still in the cardholder's possession.
- Whether the cardholder has an account with the merchant, and if so when it was created.
- Delivery address or device details from the merchant, to compare with the cardholder's own.
- Any other unrecognised charges on the card around the same date.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder raises the dispute within 90 days of the transaction date.
