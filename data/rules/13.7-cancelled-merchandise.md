# 13.7 Cancelled Merchandise or Services

## When it applies

The cardholder cancelled a one-off order, booking or service in line with the merchant's cancellation policy, and was still charged. There was only ever one order.

## When it does not apply

- The cardholder cancelled an ongoing subscription or membership and billing continued: use 13.2. A site calling a single order a subscription does not make it recurring.
- After the cancellation the merchant confirmed a refund in writing, but it never arrived: use 13.6.
- The cardholder never received the goods and did not cancel: use 13.1.

## Evidence the analyst should request

- The merchant's cancellation policy as shown at the time of purchase.
- The date and method of cancellation, and any cancellation confirmation.
- Whether the goods were shipped or the service used after cancelling.

## Time limits (illustrative)

These numbers are invented for this project. Real limits come from the card network's current rules.

- The cardholder raises the dispute within 90 days of the cancellation date.
