# Choosing between codes

Some claims fit two codes. In each pair below, one stated fact decides the code. If the claim does not show that fact, choose the code whose conditions the claim actually shows, and ask for the evidence that would settle it.

## 13.1 or 13.6

Did the merchant confirm a refund in writing? Yes: 13.6. A refund that was only mentioned, conditional or promised by phone: 13.1.

## 13.2 or 13.7

Was there an ongoing subscription or membership? Yes: 13.2. Only ever one order, whatever the merchant calls it: 13.7.

## 13.2 or 10.4

Did the cardholder ever sign up, even for a free trial they forgot, and then cancel? Yes: 13.2. Never signed up or gave their card: 10.4.

## 12.5 or 12.6

How many charges are on the statement? One, for the wrong amount: 12.5. Two or more for one purchase: 12.6.

## 13.3 or 13.1

Did anything arrive? Yes, but wrong, damaged or a cheaper model: 13.3. Nothing arrived: 13.1.

## 13.6 or 13.7

After the cancellation, did the merchant confirm a refund in writing? Yes: 13.6. No: 13.7.
